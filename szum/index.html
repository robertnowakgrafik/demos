An identity made of noise.

Szum means noise. Its logo is never drawn: four thousand lines follow a noise field, slow down inside the letters, and the name appears where they pile up. Type a line-up and every format regenerates from it.

Three rules hold the system together.

  1. Never draw the name

    The letters are only a map. Lines crossing them slow to a fifth of their speed and turn into a near-horizontal weave, so they leave five times as much ink there and the word builds up by itself.

  2. One seed, every format

    The line-up is hashed into a seed. Poster, post and story share it, so they look like siblings even though every line is different.

  3. Same input, same poster

    A seeded generator (mulberry32) and seeded Perlin noise make every poster reproducible, so a designer can fix a file months later by typing the same line-up.

Szum

Szum is a fictional festival and its line-up is invented, made for a design demo. The generator runs entirely in your browser: Perlin noise, a flow field and a text mask on canvas, with no images, libraries or AI models involved.
