Kadr 50 f/1.2

A manual-focus 50 mm lens with eleven rounded blades, for the light just before it runs out.

Reserve a lensTry the aperture

Focused by hand, to the millimetre.

A 270° focus throw from 0.45 m to infinity. The distance and depth-of-field scales are engraved and filled, so they outlast the paint.

Ten elements in seven groups.

Two aspherical surfaces and a floating rear group keep a portrait at full aperture sharp, even at 45 cm.

f/1.2

Eleven curved blades. The opening stays round down to f/2.8, then closes to a fine polygon at f/16. Keep scrolling to stop down.

640g

Brass helicoid, aluminium barrel, 72 mm from mount to filter thread.

Made in batches of 500.

Batch 01 is assembled and tested by hand, one lens at a time. Each one ships with its own MTF chart.

Kadr

Kadr is a fictional optics brand made for a design and engineering demo. The lens, its specifications and batch are invented; the depth-of-field maths is real. The 3D lens is modelled entirely in code with Three.js: no photos, no 3D files. Reservation is a demo and sends nothing.
