Set twice, turned out once.

  1. Panna cotta first

    Elderflower cream sets in a fluted tin for four hours at 4 °C, until the surface holds a fingerprint.

  2. Fruit at body heat

    The kisiel layer is poured at 30 °C, warm enough to bond, cool enough not to melt the cream beneath.

  3. Ten flutes, one tap

    A ten-second dip in hot water, a plate on top, one turn. The flutes are what make it ring when it wobbles.

Kisiel

Kisiel is a fictional jelly bar made for a design and engineering demo; the menu and prices are invented. The jelly is a real-time soft-body simulation: a lattice of particles joined into tetrahedra, solved with XPBD so it keeps its volume while it bends. Rendering in Three.js. The order form is a demo and sends nothing.
